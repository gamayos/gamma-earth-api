Gamma Earth Pricing Regions

Version 2.0 — 19 August 2026

Countries and territories are grouped into three pricing Regions. The Region determines how many Credits are consumed per km² of processing, as set out in the Fee Policy:

RegionCredits per km²km² per Credit
A1.001.00
B0.751.33
C0.502.00

The applicable Region for a processing job is determined by the country in which the submitted Area of Interest (AOI) is located. Where an AOI spans multiple countries or Regions, the Region of the centroid of the AOI applies. Gamma Earth reserves the right to update this schedule with advance notice to registered Users.
